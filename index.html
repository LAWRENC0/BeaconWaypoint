Take or choose a photo

Take a photo of the area where you are standing, facing the direction you want to search. The beacon will be drawn on this picture.

Name what you are looking for

Type a name or address. Pick the match that is the right one. Searching for "Colosseum" or "Via Appia 10, Rome" both work.

    Where are you standing?

    Your GPS position is needed to work out the direction and distance to the target. Stand where you took the photo.

    Aim the photo

    Tell the app how the camera was pointing when you took the photo. Move the sliders until the beacon sits where the target really is. The preview updates as you move them.

    Heading 0°

    The compass direction the camera faced. 0° = north, 90° = east, 180° = south, 270° = west. If you have a compass on this phone, press "Use live compass" to copy it.

    Pitch 0°

    How far the camera was tilted up or down. 0° = held level, positive = pointing up (for tall buildings), negative = pointing down.

    Field of view 70°

    How wide the lens sees. About 70° is a typical phone camera. Bigger means wider view. If you know your phone's value, use it.

    Target height 0 m

    How much higher the target is than your eyes, in metres. Leave it at 0 if you don't know. It only changes how high the beacon sits.

    Your beacon

    The red dot marks where the target is in your photo. If it is outside the frame, the message tells you which way to turn.
